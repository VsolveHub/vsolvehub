# VSolveHub — Product Overview

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** June 2026 | **Owner:** VSolveHub Product Team

---

## 1. Product Vision

> *"All services at your doorstep — trusted, fast, and fair — for every Indian city."*

VSolveHub eliminates the chaos of finding reliable local workers. No middlemen calling back in three days, no ghosting after the first call, no awkward bargaining at the door. A customer in Warangal should book a certified electrician as effortlessly as ordering food on Swiggy — and a skilled plumber in Karimnagar should have a steady flow of verified, paid work arriving on his phone without hustle.

We are building the **Urban Company for Tier 2/3 India** — with the trust infrastructure of Rapido (verified worker onboarding), the discovery of Swiggy (rich category browsing), and the simplicity demanded by first-generation smartphone users.

---

## 2. Mission Statement

> *Digitise the local services economy for Tier 2/3 Indian cities — making every service discoverable, every worker employable, and every booking trackable.*

## 3. Product Principles

| # | Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|-----------|--------------------------|
| 1 | **Mobile-first, always** | Every screen is designed for a 375px phone first. Desktop is an enhancement. |
| 2 | **Trust is earned before a job is given** | No unverified worker reaches a customer. Document review is mandatory. |
| 3 | **Every state must be communicated** | Pending, confirmed, on-the-way, done — customers and workers always know what's happening via WhatsApp + push. |
| 4 | **Speed is a feature** | Booking form < 2 minutes. OTP in < 30 seconds. Admin assignment < 5 minutes. |
| 5 | **Simplicity over completeness** | One tap to WhatsApp beats a 10-field form. Show less, do more. |
| 6 | **Scalable by design** | Adding a new service category should require zero code changes. Config drives the catalog. |

## 7. Product Scope

### Phase 1 — Direct Worker Booking (Months 1–3)
**Goal:** MVP live, first 200 bookings, 100 verified captains.
**Scope = direct booking of workers only** — dispatch model (Captain mode, Archetypes
A & B). Categories 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 (transport/wash), 10. See `conventions.md` D-27.

| Component | Scope |
|-----------|-------|
| Web App | Phase-1 (dispatch) category pages + service detail, **direct booking** form, OTP, confirm, track, FAQ, gallery, about, contact |
| Admin Panel | **Operations/monitoring** dashboard, captain verification queue, **dispatch monitor (read-only + exception force-assign)**, dispute resolution, content — **NOT per-booking assignment** (auto-dispatch handles it, D-20) |
| Provider App | **Captain mode only** (Flutter, D-23): register, KYC, verification gate, ONLINE toggle, 30s job alerts, job flow, earnings |
| Customer App | Browse, **direct book**, track, profile, push (Flutter) |
| Backend | Auto-dispatch engine (`job_assignments`, server 30s timer, waves), notifications (WhatsApp/SMS/FCM/email) |
| Integrations | WhatsApp (WATI), SMS/OTP (MSG91+DLT), FCM, Maps, GA4, reCAPTCHA |
| SEO | Sitemap, robots.txt, schema, meta on all public pages |

### Phase 2 — Non-Dispatch Archetypes + Partner Portal (Months 4–6)
- **Staffing (C):** maid/cook/driver/security placement
- **Rental (D):** equipment/furniture/vehicle inventory + availability
- **Property (E):** PG/rooms/flats listings + viewing/token
- **Events (F):** photography/decor/catering quote flow
- **Partner Web Portal** (`partner.vsolvehub.com`) + Provider App modes 2–5
- **Inbox-Pull** lead mechanic (`provider_requests`)
- Tutors & Classes; advanced search; wallet/referral; admin analytics; CSV export

### Phase 3 — Scale (Month 7+)
- Multi-language support (Telugu, Hindi)
- Vehicle add-on module (Bike Services + Local Transport)
- Subscription / membership plans
- Payment gateway integration (Razorpay/PhonePe)
- Worker earnings auto-disbursement (UPI/bank transfer)
- Multi-city expansion beyond Telangana

## 8. Out of Scope (Phase 1)

- ❌ **Mobility / rides** — auto + bike-taxi (live GPS metering; deferred, see D-22 reserved range)
- ❌ **Health-at-Home, Professional Services** expansion verticals (deferred; Tutors & Classes is the one Phase-2 expansion)
- ❌ Payment gateway / online payments (wallet UI exists, gateway is Phase 3 — but **amounts are recorded from day one**, D-08)
- ❌ Multilingual content entry (Telugu/Hindi text — design supports it, content Phase 3)
- ❌ In-platform video calling
- ❌ Worker GPS **real-time map tracking** (proximity matching uses last-known location pings, not a live map)
- ❌ Franchise / partner onboarding portal
- ❌ B2B bulk service contracts

> ✅ **Now IN scope (core, not deferred):** automated worker↔customer matching /
> auto-dispatch. This was previously listed out-of-scope; the direct-booking pivot
> (D-20) makes it the heart of the platform.
