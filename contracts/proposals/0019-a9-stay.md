# Proposal 0019 — A9 Stay dual-mode (RESERVATION + ENQUIRY)

**Author:** Cursor session (M3 Part A huge chunk), M3 FREEZE extension
**Date:** 15 September 2026 (updated 27 September 2026)
**Status:** **Implemented.**
**MVP choice:** Enquiry-first `Property` + `Unit` + `StayEnquiry` inbox.
**M3 FREEZE extension:** Dual booking_mode on `A9VariantConfiguration`:
- `RESERVATION` (paid): Hotels, Short Stay, Premium Stays, Hostels
- `ENQUIRY` (visit-first): PG, Rooms, Bed Space, Co-Living, Student/Worker Accommodation,
  Apartments, Rental Properties, Property Listing

New `StayReservation` + `UnitReservation` with date-overlap protection via `select_for_update`.
New `A9_STAY_RESERVATION` payment purpose with capture hook confirming the reservation.

## Product law

Stay ≠ visit Booking. Fail closed without ACTIVE `A9VariantConfiguration` + approved `A9_STAY`.
`booking_mode` is set per-variant-configuration and inherited by all properties on that variant.

## Payment purpose

`A9_STAY_RESERVATION` — checkout-free standalone payment. On capture, the reservation
transitions from `HELD` → `CONFIRMED`. Partner then manages check-in / check-out.

## StayReservation statuses

`HELD` → `PAYMENT_PENDING` → `CONFIRMED` → `CHECKED_IN` → `CHECKED_OUT`
Side states: `CANCELLED`, `NO_SHOW`
