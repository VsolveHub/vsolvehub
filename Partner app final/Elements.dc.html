5 · The rules every screen obeys
The first line is what the job is Service name at 16/600 on every card, 19/700 in every header. A price for an unnamed job is not a decision anyone can make.
Every button states its verb Icon, then the verb, in one to three words. No control that changes server state is ever a bare glyph.
Opposite actions never share a shape Filled and centred means go. Outlined and left‑aligned means think. Red and last means you lose something.
Numbers stay the biggest thing Digits and the rupee sign are what everyone in this market reads. The label is a third the size of the figure and never competes with it.
