## Merge rules

1. **One service per job a customer asks for.** Sizes, types, occasions and body areas become priced options inside it (for example, 17 photography occasions → 4 services).
2. **A job listed in two places is kept once**, in the place a customer would look first.
3. **Day-hire roles:** the role is the service. Tasks under a role (e.g. the 10 Mason tasks) become "what work?" choices.
4. **Rows that were not services:**
   - The 24 "BASIC VISIT CHARGE" rows → an **Inspection visit** at the top of each repair tile (in salon: a minimum order fee).
   - Bridal package contents → shown inside each package.
   - "Site visit & consultation" → the visit-fee step of the quote flow.
   - "Property Listing" (9 rows of "X for rent") → the **List your property** button.
5. **Tiny tiles join their closest neighbour.** No category is removed.
6. **Same name, different job — kept separate:** battery replacement (mobile, watch, vehicle), screen replacement (mobile, laptop), camera repair (mobile, CCTV), filter replacement (RO, vehicle).

Text in brackets = options inside that service.
