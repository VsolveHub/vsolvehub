## CHANGE REQUEST (CR)

Only **Major Changes** after approved scope are CRs (New Categories, Modules, Workflows, Third-Party Integrations, New Payment Methods, Major UI/UX Redesign, AI Expansion, Architecture Changes, New Reports/Dashboards). **Minor Changes** (bug fixes, alignment, button position, text/color/font, minor validation/usability) are not CRs. Major CRs require mutual written approval for cost and timeline.
