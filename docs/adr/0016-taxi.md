# 16. VSolve Taxi: Rental, Outstation and Book for later

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-11-27

## Context

The owner wants taxi services next to Rides and Pick up & Drop: more car types, hourly rentals with
a driver, fixed-price outstation trips, and booking any of them for later. They ship on the Rides
engine (ADR-0014, ADR-0015), with no new store build.

## Decision

See `docs/taxi/README.md` §2 (D1–D20). In short:

- `VehicleType` gains `EV_CAB`, `PREMIUM`, `SUV`, `INNOVA`, `TEMPO`. Matching is by exact type.
- `RideKind` gains `RENTAL` and `OUTSTATION`, each with its own `RideRateCard`. A check constraint
  allows only the D3 vehicle and kind pairs, and the price fields each kind uses.
- A rental has no drop. It is priced as a package (2 h / 20 km, 4 h / 40 km, 8 h / 80 km), plus extra
  km from the GPS track and extra minutes, worked out at End trip.
- An outstation trip is at least 40 km by road and has a fixed price, never re-priced by GPS.
- `RideState` gains `BOOKED`: not open and not tracked. A scheduler starts the normal search 60
  minutes before pickup and retries until 15 minutes before it.
- Contract `1.120.0`; the API is `docs/taxi/README.md` §4.4.

## Consequences

- No new service, app, native module or payment path. The fare engine, dispatch and trip screens
  now serve four kinds, so their tests must cover rentals and outstation trips.
- The drop is optional on quotes and rides. Code that reads it must allow for a rental.
- A booked trip that finds no captain still ends `NO_CAPTAIN_FOUND`, as in Rides. There is no
  guarantee of a captain at the booked time.
- Tolls, parking, permits and airport entry are paid by the rider on the way, outside our fare.
- Cancellation fees, night charges, online payment and corporate billing are not in this version.
